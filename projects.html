Our process

How a project comes to life

A community names the need

Not a survey written elsewhere. A conversation with the people who live with the problem every day.

We scope it honestly

What would actually solve this, what would it cost, who maintains it afterwards, and what happens if we get it wrong?

Funding is raised transparently

Donations, events, and partnerships — with a clear line between what was given and what it went towards.

Delivery with local partners

Local knowledge leads. Our role is to resource the work and stay accountable for it.

We report back

Including the parts that did not go to plan. Trust is built by what you admit, not only by what you announce.
