Our purpose

Every person deserves to live with dignity

At I Am Human, we believe that every person deserves to live with dignity, justice, and freedom, regardless of race, gender, nationality, or background. Our goal is simple yet powerful: to promote and protect human rights in Africa and around the world.

Human rights go beyond basic freedoms. Every person deserves clean drinking water, education, healthcare, and the minimum necessities to survive and live a dignified life. This is where I Am Human comes into play.

Human rights and basic needs should not be ideals; they should be a reality for all.

What we believe

Four convictions behind everything we do

  • Dignity is not earned

    It belongs to every person from the beginning, and no authority, circumstance, or border can take it away.

  • Equality without exception

    Race, gender, nationality, and background change nothing about what a person is owed. Every life has equal value.

  • Justice needs accountability

    Rights that cannot be enforced are only promises. Where power is abused, someone has to be willing to say so out loud.

  • Solidarity, not charity

    Communities are not problems to be solved. They are partners who know what they need and deserve a say in how it is delivered.

How we work

Shaped around people, not templates

Every country, region, and community faces a different reality. Our projects are built to fit them, rather than asking them to fit us.

Listen first

We start by asking a community what it actually needs, not by arriving with a plan already written.

Design together

Projects are shaped with local people, local knowledge, and local leadership, so that what we build belongs to them.

Deliver the essentials

Clean water, healthcare, education, and dignified living conditions, the minimum necessities for a life of dignity.

Stay accountable

We report openly on what we raise, what we spend, and what changes, including when something does not go to plan.
