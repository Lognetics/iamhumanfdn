Our conviction

Why Second Chance?

Because every human being deserves a chance to rebuild.

At I Am Human Foundation, we believe that no matter what has happened to you, you remain human — and you deserve dignity, rights, and the opportunity to move forward.

I know this personally. After experiencing the violation of my own human rights in Ghana, I was given a second chance to rebuild my life, reclaim my voice, and continue my journey. That experience deepened my belief that one difficult chapter should never define an entire lifetime.

A mistake should not define a lifetime.

A circumstance should not determine a person’s worth.

Every human being deserves the right to rebuild.

Second Chance exists to help remove the barriers that prevent people from rebuilding their lives with dignity.

The programme

What does a second chance look like?

I Am Human will support a small number of carefully selected individuals whose ability to live with dignity and exercise their rights has been significantly disrupted.

  • Reintegration

    Supporting someone returning to society after incarceration or a major life setback.

  • Health

    Helping someone access essential medical treatment that is financially out of reach.

  • Education

    Giving someone the opportunity to continue their education, gain a qualification, or develop new skills.

  • Rebuilding

    Helping someone regain the ability to work, become independent, and rebuild their life.

We don’t simply provide financial assistance. We identify the barrier standing between a person and a dignified future — and help remove it.

Beyond financial giving

Open a Door

Not every contribution has to be financial. A second chance can begin with access.

  • Employ

    Offering an internship or employment opportunity

  • Educate

    Providing training, certification, or educational support

  • Mentor

    Offering professional guidance and mentorship

  • Provide

    Contributing medical, legal, or professional services

  • Connect

    Opening doors through your network and relationships

  • Amplify

    Using your platform to raise awareness and extend our reach

Sometimes the most valuable thing you can give someone isn’t money. It’s access.
